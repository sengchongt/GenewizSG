Gene Synthesis

Choose fragments or cloned synthetic genes.

GENEWIZ synthesis services support custom DNA needs from linear fragments through cloned genes, with project updates and design support available.

FragmentGENE

Double-stranded linear DNA fragments from 100-3,000 bp, delivered dried for resuspension, cloning, and screening.

  • 500 ng to 1,000 ng deliverable yield by length
  • Typical completion from 2-8 business days by size
  • Useful for rapid iteration, assembly, and screening

PriorityGENE

Standard gene synthesis with cloning into a plasmid for custom synthetic sequences.

  • Clonal genes in roughly 5-8 business days, depending on region and project
  • 2-5 ug plasmid DNA deliverable
  • Includes sequence verification and project documentation
